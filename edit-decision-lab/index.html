    Part 2 · Log and select

    Choose material for what it contributes

    Review the fictional clips from After the Tide. Include or remove each clip, name its possible function, and leave an editor note.

    Part 3 · Build a scene

    Let action, interruption, and consequence develop

    Move included clips earlier or later. The aim is not to match a hidden answer; it is to create an intelligible, honest dramatic event.

    Part 4 · Shape the rough cut

    Arrange scenes around a developing question

    Move the four scene cards, then name the evidence, pickups, sound, and context the next cut requires.

    Part 5 · Review and export

    Write the memo for the next cut

    Describe the film that has emerged, the perspective shaping it, the ethical choice to protect, and the most important revision.
